Course Code: ESC2101P04 Experiment No. 01

Verification of Law of Polygon of Forces

1. Aim & Theory

Aim: To verify graphically and analytically the equilibrium of a particle using the Law of Polygon of Forces.

"If a number of concurrent forces are acting simultaneously on a body or a particle, and are represented in magnitude and direction by the sides of a polygon taken in order, then their resultant is represented in magnitude and direction by the closing side of the polygon taken in opposite order."

When a particle subjected to multiple concurrent forces is in static equilibrium, the resultant force is zero (ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0), forming a closed force polygon. Any non-zero gap is recorded as the error of closure, R.

2. Pre-requisites & Apparatus

Pre-requisites:

  • Concept of concurrent coplanar force systems and static equilibrium.
  • Resolution of forces into orthogonal components Fx = F cos θ and Fy = F sin θ.
  • Graphical vector addition using the head-to-tail polygon method.

Apparatus: Universal Force Table, slotted weights with hangers/pans, frictionless pulleys, inextensible threads, central ring, white drawing sheet, and spirit level.

3. Procedure (Lab & Virtual Simulation)
  1. Level the Universal Force Table using a spirit level and fix the pulleys along the graduated circular rim at desired angles θ1, θ2...
  2. Attach threads to the central ring, pass each thread over its respective frictionless pulley, and suspend suitable slotted weights F = mg at the free ends.
  3. Adjust the weights and pulley angles until the central ring is centered and stationary (achieving static equilibrium).
  4. Note down the hanging weights and their angles of inclination with respect to the X axis.
  5. Construct the Space Diagram and draw the Vector Polygon to a suitable scale. Verify that the polygon closes (or measure the graphical error of closure).
  6. Compute ΣFx, ΣFy,
    Resultant R = √(ΣFx)2 + (ΣFy)2, and
    direction θ = tan-1|ΣFy/ ΣFx|
    analytically, then lock the observation to record up to 4 trials.
4. Precautions
  • Ensure the Universal Force Table is strictly horizontal using a spirit level before taking readings.
  • Check that all pulleys rotate freely with minimal friction so tension equals the suspended weight.
  • Ensure the central ring does not touch the central pin when recording equilibrium positions.
  • Avoid parallax error while reading pulley angles on the circular degree scale.
  • Keep weights hanging still without swinging before locking an observation.
